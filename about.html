About us

Twelve years of going and preaching.

Christ Gospel Church is a congregation in Cheran Ma Nagar, Coimbatore, built around the Bible, the worship of Jesus Christ, and care for people.

Our story

A new beginning, again.

In September 2026 we gave thanks for twelve years of God's faithfulness. The theme of that anniversary was New Beginning, taken from Isaiah 43:19.

Over those years the church has grown beyond its own walls. Sunday services are streamed live, and Rev. Dr. P. Chandran's daily 5-Minute Meditation reaches people wherever they are.
